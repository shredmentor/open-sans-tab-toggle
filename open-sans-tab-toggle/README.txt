OPEN SANS TAB TOGGLE

INSTALL
1. Unzip the archive.
2. In Chrome open chrome://extensions
3. Turn on Developer mode.
4. Click Load unpacked and choose the open-sans-tab-toggle folder.
5. Pin the extension to the toolbar (puzzle-piece icon).

USE
- Click the extension icon to enable Open Sans in the current tab.
- The ON badge means it is active.
- Click again to restore the site's fonts.
- Reloading or navigating the tab resets the toggle.

NOTES
- Uses Google Fonts to load Open Sans if the font is not installed locally; internet access may be required.
- Pages such as chrome://, the Chrome Web Store, and certain protected pages block extensions.
- Forcing font families may change some icon fonts into ordinary characters.
